Concept Project

EventsDock — Launch Campaign

A speculative go-to-market plan for EventsDock: who to reach, what to say, and how to know if it worked.

RoleGrowth strategy & content planning
Format3-week campaign
TypeConcept project — speculative

objective

Objective & audience

Generate the first 100 organic sign-ups in the four weeks after launch, with a focus on the people most likely to feel EventsDock's core pain point immediately.

Audience: couples planning weddings (25–35), parents organizing birthday parties, and small businesses running corporate events — all active on Instagram and TikTok, all currently managing guest lists in spreadsheets or group chats.

strategy

Three content pillars

01

Build in public

Behind-the-scenes of building the product — earns trust before there's anything to sell.

02

Feature demos

Short, concrete demos of the features that save the most time — guest import, WhatsApp RSVP.

03

Real use cases

"How X organized Y" stories — turns features into a picture of the actual outcome.
